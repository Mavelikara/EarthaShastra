Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Eartha Shastra products and our practices.

About Our Products
What makes Eartha Shastra products different?
Our products combine 5,000 years of Ayurvedic wisdom with modern scientific research. Every ingredient is carefully selected for purity and efficacy, and our formulas are free from parabens, sulfates, and artificial fragrances.
Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?
Yos! Most of our products are formulated for all skin types, including sensitive skin. Look for our “Sensitive Skin — Approved” label on products specially formulated for delicate skin.
Are your products cruelty-free and vegan?
Absolutely. All Eartha Shastra products are 100% cruelty-free and never tested on animals. The majority of our range is also vegan - look for the Vegan label on individual products.
Do you use artificial preservatives?
No. We use only natural preservatives like tocopherol (Vitamin E), rosemary extract, and nim extracts. Our products are free from parabens, phenoxyethanol, and synthetic preservatives.
Orders & Shipping

Returns & Refunds
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. If you're not satisfied for any reason, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll arrange a free pickup and full refund.
How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the returned item. The amount will be credited to your original payment method.
Ingredients & Safety
Where do you source your ingredients?
Our ingredients are sourced from certified organic farms across India, primarily from Kerala, Uttarakhand, and Rajasthan. We work directly with farmers to ensure quality and fair pricing.
Are your products dermatologist tested?
Yos! All our products undergo rigorous dermatological testing and are approved by leading dermatologists. We also carry GMP and Ayush certifications.
Can I use your products during pregnancy?
While many of our products are safe during pregnancy, we always recommend consulting your healthcare provider first. Some essential oil-based products should be avoided.

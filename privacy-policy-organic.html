Collection of Information

We collect personal information that helps us identify you and serve you better when you interact with our website or services. This information may be collected during registration, order placement, customer support interactions, or other engagements with Earthashastra.

The information we may collect includes, but is not limited to:

  • Personal details such as name, age, address, phone number, email address, occupation, and proof of identity or address.
  • Sensitive personal data such as payment details (bank account, credit/debit card information), passwords, or other payment-related credentials.
  • Usage-related information including browsing activity, purchase history, preferences, device details, location data, call records, and other information you voluntarily provide.

You may browse certain sections of our website without revealing personal information. However, once you provide personal data, you are no longer anonymous to us. Mandatory and optional fields are clearly indicated wherever possible. You always have the choice not to provide information by choosing not to use specific services or features.

We may automatically collect certain technical and behavioral information such as IP address, browser type, referring URLs, and website navigation patterns. This information is used in aggregated form to analyze trends, improve services, and enhance user experience.

Information that is publicly available or disclosed under applicable laws, including the Right to Information Act, 2005, is not considered sensitive personal information.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. Cookies are small data files stored on your device that help us improve website functionality and performance. Cookies do not store personal information directly.

We use cookies to:

  • Deliver content relevant to your interests

Most cookies are session-based and are automatically deleted when you close your browser. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may limit certain features of the website.

Third-party cookies may also be placed on some pages. Earthashastra does not control or take responsibility for such cookies.

Products

What are your products made from?

Our range is built around gaumay — cow dung — combined with natural resins, herbs and botanicals such as guggal, camphor and sandalwood. We use no synthetic charcoal, no chemical fragrance and no artificial binders.

What are the four product categories?
  • Wellness — dhoop, sambrani and havan essentials for daily ritual.
  • Beauty — skin and body preparations made with natural ingredients.
  • Personal Care — everyday care products for the body.
  • Home Care — products for cleaning, purifying and caring for the home.
Is there a smell of cow dung?

No. The gaumay is processed, dried and blended with natural resins and botanicals, so what you smell when burning is the herbs and resins — not the base material.

Are the products safe for daily use indoors?

Yes. Because there is no synthetic charcoal or chemical fragrance, the smoke is far cleaner than conventional dhoop. As with any incense, use in a ventilated space and never leave a burning product unattended.

Why do colour, texture and fragrance vary between batches?

Every product is handmade from natural materials, so slight variation between batches is expected and is not a defect. It is the same reason no two handmade pieces are ever identical.

Do you test on animals?

No. Nothing we make is tested on animals, and no animal is harmed anywhere in our process.

Use & Storage

How do I light and use sambrani cups and havan discs?

Place the cup or disc on a heatproof plate or holder, light the edge until it catches, and let the flame settle into a slow smoulder. Keep it away from curtains and paper, and never leave it burning unattended.

How long does each product burn?

Burn time varies by product and by conditions such as airflow and humidity. Approximate burn times are noted on each product page.

How should I store the products?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Because the products are natural and unpreserved, humidity is the main thing to protect them from.

Is there a shelf life?

Where a product has a recommended use-by period, it is printed on the packaging. Stored dry and away from sunlight, the ritual range keeps well for a long time.

What do I do with the ash?

The ash is fully natural and can go into your garden soil or potted plants.

Sustainability & Impact

Where do you source your materials?

Who makes the products?

Is the packaging recyclable?

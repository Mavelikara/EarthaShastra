About Us

Rituals rooted in what the earth already gives.

EarthaShastra began with a simple question: why has one of nature's oldest, most self-sustaining materials — cow dung — been pushed out of modern life? We set out to bring it back, cleaned up and made ready for everyday ritual.

How We Started

From a village workshop to a household ritual.

We started by working directly with rural artisans who already understood this material better than any lab could. What began as a handful of dhoop sticks made for family use grew into a full range of pooja essentials — always made the same way, just more of it.

What We Believe

Simplicity is the whole point.

No synthetic charcoal. No chemical fragrance. No overproduction. Every product we make is built the way it always should have been — with fewer ingredients, and more intention.

Operations - Brand Architect

A marketing buff since childhood, she grew up fascinated by the iconic advertising of the ’90s and early 2000s. Television, print and outdoor campaigns left a lasting impression on her, not just the catchy jingles, but the branding behind them. Colours, fonts, visual styles and the little details that made a brand instantly recognisable were always her thing.

That fascination led her to pursue a PGDM in Marketing Communications and eventually build six to seven years of experience across social media marketing, branding and video production.

But somewhere along the way, she realised that a career and experience alone weren’t enough. Her biggest driver has always been purpose. She needs to understand the why behind what she does, and for those whys to resonate with her before she can truly feel settled and fulfilled.

Se believes in wearing her heart on her sleeve-being genuine, expressive and deeply invested in the things and people that matter to her. At the same time, she believes strongly in balance: between heart and head, passion and perspective, purpose and practicality.

Our Journey

Born From Belief

Eartha Shastra began in a small kitchen in Kerala, where our founders blended generational ayurvedic recipes with a passion for pure, healing ingredients.

What started as a personal quest for better wellness products has grown into a movement that touches lives across India and beyond.

Every product we create is a pledge: to honor tradition, embrace science, and deliver results you can feel.
